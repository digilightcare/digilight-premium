This policy describes the website-level cookie and tracking practices that may apply on digilightindia.com. Individual products may publish separate cookie notices and controls.
Effective date: 23 November 2025 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, local storage and scripts that recognise a browser or measure activity. They may be set by Digilight India or by third-party services embedded in the website.

2. Categories and purposes

  • Essential and security: support basic website delivery, navigation, load balancing, fraud prevention and security.
  • Analytics and performance: help understand page visits, traffic sources, interactions and website performance.
  • Advertising and attribution: help measure visits or actions associated with advertising campaigns and understand campaign performance.
  • Embedded and communication tools: support newsletter forms, external media, fonts or other third-party website features.

3. Third-party technologies

The website source currently includes Google Tag Manager and Google measurement/advertising event integrations, as well as MailerLite newsletter functionality. Google Fonts and Font Awesome may load resources from their respective providers. These services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy and cookie documentation. The exact cookies may change as website tags and vendors are updated.

4. Your controls

You can block, delete or restrict cookies using your browser settings. Some browser extensions and provider tools may also allow you to limit analytics or advertising tracking. Blocking cookies may affect some website features. Where a consent choice or preference tool is presented, use it to manage the relevant optional technologies.

5. Updates and related policy

We may update this policy when website technologies or legal requirements change. For details about personal information more generally, read the Privacy Policy. A product-specific cookie notice applies to the relevant product where published.
