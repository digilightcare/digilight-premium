# Customer Website Legal Pages Setup

Internal implementation checklist for Digilight India website projects. This is an operational workflow, not legal advice or a substitute for customer-specific legal review.

## 1. Identify the contracting customer
- Confirm the business/individual name that will operate the website.
- Record business address, support email, phone and applicable registration details supplied by the customer.
- Confirm the customer is authorised to approve the website and its legal content.
- Do not identify Digilight India as the website operator unless that is expressly part of the contracted service.

## 2. Understand the website
Record which features are actually included:
- Enquiry/contact forms and WhatsApp/call links.
- Online payments, deposits, subscriptions, refunds or cancellations.
- Products, delivery, shipping, returns or booking services.
- Customer accounts, login, user-generated content or memberships.
- Cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, newsletter tools and embedded media.
- Collection or processing of personal, financial, health, identity or other sensitive information.
- Third-party services, processors, platforms and integrations.

## 3. Select only applicable legal pages
Typical starting set, subject to the business model and applicable law:
- Terms & Conditions.
- Privacy Policy.
- Refund & Cancellation Policy.
- Shipping / Delivery / Returns Policy, where relevant.
- Cookie Policy, where tracking technologies are used.
- Additional sector-specific notices or annexures where applicable.

Do not publish a generic policy merely to fill a footer. If a policy is not relevant, document why it is not included.

## 4. Draft from verified facts
- Use the customer's supplied business details and actual website functionality.
- Identify assumptions and unanswered questions instead of inventing answers.
- Do not copy Digilight India's own policies and simply replace the business name.
- Do not promise legal compliance, guaranteed enforceability or regulator approval.
- For regulated activities, identify the relevant professional/institutional requirements and request customer or qualified-adviser review.

## 5. Customer approval before launch
Send the complete staging website and all proposed legal pages to the customer.
Obtain written approval from an authorised owner/representative by email or another retained communication channel.
Record:
- Customer and authorised approver.
- Website/domain and staging URL.
- Exact legal page names and versions reviewed.
- Date and time of approval.
- Any corrections or conditions requested.
- Confirmation that the customer has checked business facts, service descriptions, prices, claims, privacy disclosures, refund practices, licences and sector-specific requirements.

Do not treat silence, payment alone, or a general verbal discussion as approval of final legal content.

## 6. Publish and retain records
- Publish only the approved version.
- Save the approval communication and final policy versions with the project record.
- Record launch date and deployed website URL.
- Treat material post-approval changes to legal pages, business model, data collection, payments or regulated services as requiring renewed customer review.
- Hand over the final URLs and copies to the customer.

## 7. Responsibility boundary
Digilight India implements the agreed website and legal-page content based on information and instructions supplied by the customer. The customer is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of its business information, legal obligations, licences, service classifications, claims, customer-facing practices and final approval. Digilight India does not act as the customer's lawyer, accountant, regulator or professional adviser. This operational allocation does not exclude any duty or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 8. Category-specific annexures
Where a business category is subject to additional institutional, statutory or professional requirements, prepare a separate project annexure/checklist identifying:
- The applicable authority, rules and version/date reviewed.
- Which website content, services, claims, contact methods or functionality are affected.
- Customer confirmations and evidence required.
- Items requiring approval by the customer's qualified professional or institution.
- The final annexure version and customer acceptance record.

The annexure supplements the main website project agreement; it should not silently override the accepted scope, payment schedule or general terms.
