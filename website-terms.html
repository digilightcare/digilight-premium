Website Design & Development Terms

Clear scope. Transparent payments. Written approvals. These terms explain how Digilight India delivers one-page landing pages and website design services.

Important: These are the general terms for website design and development services offered by Digilight India. The project-specific quotation, approved scope, package selection and written change approvals form part of the agreement. If a signed or expressly accepted project proposal contains a specific term that differs from these general terms, the project-specific term will apply to that project to the extent of the difference. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable Indian law.

1. Service provider, acceptance and project confirmation

“Digilight”, “we”, “us” or “our” refers to Digilight India, the website design and development service provider identified in the project quotation. “Client”, “you” or “your” means the person or business placing the order.

A project is confirmed only after the client has (a) selected a package or accepted a written quotation, (b) approved the deliverables, exclusions, price and target timeline in writing (including by email or WhatsApp), and (c) paid the stated advance. Electronic acceptance and communications may be used to document the agreement. The quotation will identify the contracting/invoicing details applicable to the order.

2. Package scope and deliverables

  • Basic Landing Page: one-page website with up to 5 agreed content sections, responsive layout, WhatsApp and click-to-call links, WhatsApp-connected enquiry form, Google Maps embed where applicable, basic on-page SEO, one design revision round, deployment and 7 days of post-launch bug-fix support.
  • Premium Landing Page: one-page website with up to 8 agreed content sections, all Basic features, more customized visual design and section layouts, enhanced on-page SEO (page metadata, heading structure and image alt text), Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console setup where the client supplies required account access, up to 2 design revision rounds and 30 days of post-launch bug-fix support.
  • Both packages include first-year hosting on a supported platform, one platform-provided subdomain and SSL/HTTPS, subject to the platform's availability and terms.

The precise page structure, sections, content, integrations and delivery items will be listed in the accepted project brief. A demo or sample is illustrative; it is not a promise that every sample element, stock asset, paid feature or third-party integration is included.

3. Items not included unless separately quoted

Unless expressly included in the accepted written scope, the package excludes custom domain purchase/renewal, hosting renewal after the first year, source code or editable project files, repository or account transfer, extra pages or sections, backend/database/CRM, automated email or SMS, booking or payment gateway, login/member portals, paid plugins/APIs/subscriptions, premium stock assets, professional photography, logo creation, extensive copywriting, translation, data entry, advanced SEO campaigns, advertising, ongoing marketing, maintenance and new feature development.

Any third-party service or feature that carries a recurring or usage-based fee will be disclosed for client approval before it is purchased or activated. The client is responsible for approved third-party charges.

4. Price, taxes and payment schedule

  • Basic: introductory offer ₹4,999/- against displayed regular price ₹9,999/-. Payment: ₹2,500 advance and ₹2,499 balance.
  • Premium: introductory offer ₹9,999/- against displayed regular price ₹19,999/-. Payment: ₹5,000 advance and ₹4,999 balance.

These introductory prices apply only where confirmed in the written quotation and while the offer is available. Applicable taxes, if any, will be stated in the quotation or invoice. The client must pay the advance to reserve the project slot and start work. The remaining balance is due after the preview is made available and before production launch, domain connection, source-code release or final handover, as applicable. We may pause delivery or handover if an amount due remains unpaid.

Payments must be made only to the payment details formally communicated by Digilight India. The client should retain transaction proof and request an acknowledgement/receipt.

5. Client responsibilities and supplied content

The client must provide accurate business information, approved copy, logo, images, contact details, service descriptions, legal disclosures, credentials, testimonials and access credentials required for the project. The client confirms that it owns or has permission to use all supplied materials and that their use does not infringe third-party rights.

The client is responsible for the legality, accuracy and substantiation of claims, licences, professional qualifications, prices, offers, privacy notices, terms, regulated-industry statements and other content published on its website. Digilight may request evidence or decline to publish material that appears unlawful, misleading, infringing or unsafe. The client must review and approve the preview before launch.

Passwords should not be sent in plain text over public channels. Where possible, the client should grant role-based access and revoke temporary access after completion.

6. Timeline, dependencies and client delays

Indicative delivery targets are 2 working days for Basic and 3–5 working days for Premium, starting only after the advance is received and all required content, assets, approvals and access are available. These are estimates, not guaranteed deadlines.

Time spent waiting for client content, clarification, approvals, third-party verification, domain/DNS changes, platform review or external providers is excluded from the delivery estimate. If the client does not respond or provide required materials for 15 calendar days, the project may be placed on hold and the schedule reassigned. Restart may require a revised delivery date and, where the scope or costs have changed, a revised quotation.

7. Revisions, change requests and approval

A revision is a reasonable adjustment to the agreed design or supplied content within the approved scope. Basic includes one consolidated revision round; Premium includes up to two consolidated revision rounds. A revision round should be submitted as one clear list of changes.

New sections, new pages, additional functionality, new integrations, replacement of an approved design direction, substantial content replacement or changes requested after final approval are not included revisions. Such work will be quoted separately and may extend the timeline. Work on a change request begins only after written approval of its price and schedule.

The client is responsible for checking names, spelling, phone numbers, email addresses, legal notices, links and all content before giving launch approval.

8. Preview, testing, launch and acceptance

A preview URL is supplied for review and is not necessarily a production-ready website. Before launch, the client must approve the final preview in writing and pay all outstanding amounts. Forms, analytics, maps, WhatsApp links and other integrations will be tested only to the extent included in the agreed scope and technically accessible.

Where a form opens WhatsApp, the visitor must complete sending the message in WhatsApp; this is not an automated CRM, email inbox or database submission. Email delivery, CRM routing, payment collection and other backend functions are excluded unless specifically quoted and tested.

After launch, the client should report any material mismatch against the approved scope promptly. Use of the live site or written confirmation of acceptance will be treated as acceptance of the delivered work, without limiting rights available under law.

9. Cancellation, suspension and refunds

The client may request cancellation in writing. If cancellation occurs before work starts, Digilight will refund amounts received, less any actual, documented and non-recoverable payment or third-party charges incurred for that project. Once work has started, the advance will be applied against work completed, time reasonably committed and non-recoverable project costs. Any unearned balance, if due, will be calculated fairly and refunded after deducting those documented amounts.

If Digilight cancels a project for reasons not caused by the client, amounts received for work not performed and costs not incurred will be refunded. If a project is suspended because the client fails to provide information, approvals or payment, the schedule may be paused and any restart date will be agreed. Refunds, where payable, will normally be processed to the original payment source within 7–10 business days after the amount is agreed, subject to payment-provider timelines.

Nothing in this clause limits a consumer's statutory rights or excludes any refund or remedy required by applicable law.

10. Hosting, domain, third-party services and renewals

The first 12 months of hosting, platform subdomain and SSL are included from the first production publication, on the selected supported provider and subject to its plan, acceptable-use rules, technical limits and continued availability. A platform subdomain is not a custom domain and may include provider branding or restrictions.

Custom domains should preferably be registered in the client's own name and account. The client is responsible for domain registration details, renewal, DNS access and renewal charges. After the included 12 months, hosting, domain, SSL-related or platform charges (if any) are payable by the client at then-current provider rates. Digilight does not control third-party outages, policy changes, account suspensions, pricing changes or service discontinuation.

11. Intellectual property, portfolio and handover

Until all project invoices are paid, project-specific design and development work remains under Digilight's control and may not be copied, reused, published, modified or transferred by the client except for review of the preview. After full payment, the client receives the right to use the final delivered website design and agreed deliverables for its own business, subject to third-party licences and any written exclusions.

Complete source code, editable project files, design source files, private repositories, reusable frameworks, internal tools, credentials and transfer of hosting/deployment accounts are not included unless expressly stated in the quotation. Third-party fonts, images, libraries and software remain subject to their respective licences. Digilight may display the completed public website in its portfolio unless the parties agree in writing to confidentiality or non-display before publication.

12. Support, defects and maintenance

Basic includes 7 calendar days and Premium includes 30 calendar days of post-launch bug-fix support, starting on the production launch date. This covers reproducible defects where the delivered website materially fails to operate according to the approved scope on supported current browsers and devices.

Support does not include new features, new content, redesign, changes caused by client edits, third-party outages or updates, compromised client accounts, malware introduced through external access, domain/DNS changes, paid-service failures or issues outside Digilight's reasonable control. Ongoing maintenance and support after the included period require a separate agreement.

13. SEO, traffic and business outcomes

SEO setup means technical and on-page basics described in the selected package. Search engines independently decide whether and when to crawl, index or rank a website. Digilight does not guarantee search rankings, traffic, leads, enquiries, sales, advertising results or revenue. Analytics configuration does not guarantee data completeness and depends on correct client permissions, consent configuration and third-party systems.

14. Privacy, data and security

The client must ensure that any personal data collected through its website is collected lawfully and that appropriate privacy notices, consent language, retention practices and access controls are in place. Unless a backend/data-processing feature is separately agreed, Digilight's standard landing-page enquiry form is designed to open a WhatsApp message and does not store submissions in a Digilight database.

The client is responsible for its own website content, account security, passwords, user permissions and privacy/legal compliance after handover. Each party will use reasonable care with confidential information received from the other and will not disclose it except as needed to perform the project or as required by law.

Digilight will perform the agreed services with reasonable skill and care. Except for express commitments in the accepted scope and warranties that cannot be excluded by law, no other guarantee is made that the website will be uninterrupted, error-free on every device, immune from cyber incidents or achieve a particular commercial result.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Digilight will not be liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss, loss of profits, loss of anticipated savings, loss of data or business interruption arising from matters outside its reasonable control. For a direct claim relating to a project, Digilight's aggregate contractual liability will not exceed the fees actually paid for that project, except where liability cannot lawfully be limited, including liability arising from fraud or wilful misconduct.

Neither party is responsible for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including major network or hosting outages, natural disasters, government restrictions, strikes, war or widespread infrastructure failure. The affected party will inform the other and resume performance as soon as reasonably practicable.

These terms are governed by the laws of India. The parties will first attempt to resolve a project dispute through good-faith written discussion.
