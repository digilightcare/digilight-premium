Privacy Policy

How Digilight India collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal information.

This policy applies to personal information processed through digilightindia.com and relevant Digilight India enquiries and services where no separate product-specific privacy policy applies. A product's own privacy policy governs data processed within that product.
Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who is responsible

Digilight India is the operating name of the business that determines why and how personal information is processed for its own website, enquiries, customer relationships, billing, marketing and business administration. For a product or service, the relevant privacy notice may identify a different role or additional responsible party.

2. Information we may collect

  • Contact and identity details such as name, email address, phone number and business name.
  • Enquiry details, service interests, messages, project instructions, communications and feedback.
  • Order, quotation, invoice and payment status or transaction references. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Digilight India.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, pages viewed, referral source, approximate location derived from technical data, and interaction or diagnostic logs.
  • Newsletter subscription preferences and records of consent or unsubscribe requests.
  • Information supplied by a customer for a service engagement, which may include business or end-customer data depending on the agreed scope.

3. How information is collected

We collect information directly when you submit a contact form, email, call, message us, request a quotation, subscribe to communications, purchase a service or provide project materials. Some technical information may be collected through cookies, tags, analytics and similar technologies. Payment providers may send us payment confirmation, transaction identifiers and status.

4. Purposes and lawful basis

We use information to respond to enquiries; prepare proposals; provide and improve services; manage projects and customer accounts; process payments and invoices; provide support; secure our systems; prevent fraud and misuse; maintain business records; comply with legal obligations; and send marketing communications where permitted and, where required, with consent. We seek to process only information reasonably relevant to these purposes and rely on consent, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate uses or other lawful grounds available under applicable law.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may share relevant information with personnel, contractors and service providers who support hosting, website operations, email/newsletter delivery, analytics, advertising measurement, customer communications, payment processing, security and professional advice. Current website functionality includes Google Tag Manager/Google measurement or advertising tags and MailerLite newsletter functionality. If you make a payment, the payment provider (including Razorpay where offered) receives the information required to process it. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable arrangements.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or safety, in connection with a business transfer, or with your direction. We do not sell personal information as a standalone commercial product.

6. Cookies and analytics

Cookies and similar technologies may support site functionality, traffic measurement, campaign attribution, security and newsletter or advertising interactions. See the Cookie Policy for categories and controls. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality or measurement.

7. Cross-border processing

Some service providers may process information on infrastructure located outside India. Where this occurs, we will take steps required by applicable Indian law and relevant contractual safeguards, and will observe any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

8. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, providing services, resolving disputes, maintaining security records and meeting accounting or legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type. When information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable, subject to backups and lawful retention requirements.

9. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to information about your personal data, correction or updating, erasure where retention is no longer necessary, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, or information about how to raise a grievance. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out or records we must retain.

10. Security and children

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our services are directed to businesses and adults; do not knowingly submit personal information about a child unless legally authorised and necessary for the relevant service.

11. Product-specific privacy notices

Digilight PMS has its own privacy policy and data-processing documents because it handles hotel, staff and guest information in a product-specific context. Use the legal links on digilightpms.com for that product. Other products may publish separate privacy notices. This central policy does not replace a product-specific notice where one applies.
