Good questions

Clear answers. No surprises.

Understand exactly what the ₹4,999 package includes before you get started.

What is included in the ₹4,999 package?

One professionally designed, mobile-responsive landing page with business overview, services, WhatsApp and click-to-call links, a WhatsApp-connected enquiry form, Google Maps integration, basic on-page SEO, one revision and first-year hosting on an agreed platform subdomain.

How does the payment work?

₹2,500 is payable to start the project. The remaining ₹2,499 is payable after completion, before final handover or publishing.

How quickly will my website be ready?

The target is 48 hours after advance payment and receipt of all required business details, content, images and approvals. Delays in receiving materials or feedback can affect the timeline.

Is a custom domain name included?

Custom domain purchase and connection are not included. First-year hosting is provided on an agreed platform subdomain. We can discuss a custom domain separately.

Will my website rank first on Google?

No ethical agency can guarantee a specific Google position. The package includes basic on-page SEO foundations. Rankings depend on competition, content quality, website authority, technical performance and ongoing SEO work.

Will I receive a multi-page website or online store?

No. This offer is for one landing page. Additional pages, e-commerce, payment gateway integration, booking systems and custom functionality require a separate quote.

Will my enquiry form save customer information?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message with the details entered by the visitor. It does not store enquiries in a database or CRM.

What do you need from me to begin?

Your business name, services, city or service area, contact details, logo if available, photos and the key information you want customers to know.
