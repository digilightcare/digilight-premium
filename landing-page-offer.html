Is this a multi-page website?

No. Both offers are for a single-page website with the stated content-section limit. Additional pages or sections require a separate written quote.

Is a custom domain included?

No. First-year hosting, a supported platform subdomain and SSL are included as described in the package terms. A custom domain and renewals are separate costs.

After the package, scope and timeline are approved in writing and the advance payment is received.
