Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers before you decide.

Is ₹4,999 the final price?

Yes. ₹4,999 is the total fee for the defined one-page package. Optional extras such as a custom domain, paid hosting, additional pages, custom features and source-code handover are quoted separately before any work on those extras begins.

How does the payment work?

₹2,500 advance to begin the project and ₹2,499 after completion, before the final launch/handover.

Will my website be ready in 48 hours?

Our target is 48 hours after the advance is received and all required business content, images, contact details and approvals are supplied. Delays in receiving content or approvals can extend the timeline.

Do I get a free domain name?

A platform-provided subdomain is included. A custom domain such as yourbusiness.com is not included; domain purchase and renewal are separately chargeable.

What happens after the first year?

The included hosting period covers the first year under the agreed hosting arrangement. Any renewal or paid hosting after that period will be discussed and quoted before renewal.

Will I receive the source code?

Source-code handover is not included in the ₹4,999 package. If you need it, ask us for a separate quote before starting.

Can I request more pages or changes?

The package includes one landing page and one round of revisions. Additional pages, major redesigns and further revisions can be quoted separately.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or call. We will understand your requirements, confirm the scope and share the next steps for the advance payment.
